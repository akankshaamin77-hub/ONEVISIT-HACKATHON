How ONEVISIT works

1

Enter

Create a family profile and add the people whose circumstances matter.

2

Describe

Add occupation, age, income, location, education and other relevant circumstances.

3

Compare

ONEVISIT compares the entered information with the scheme records in its prototype database.

4

Understand

Each result explains why it appeared and which information may still need verification.

5

Prepare

The user can see documents, next steps and the official source before applying.
