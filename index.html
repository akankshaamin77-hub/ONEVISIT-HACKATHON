What Makes FAMILY360 Different?

More than a scheme search box.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Family Graph

Connect multiple family members with different occupations, ages and circumstances into one family-level profile.

🧠

Explainable Matching

Instead of only showing a scheme, FAMILY360 explains why it appeared and what information still needs verification.

💬

Situation Assistant

Users can describe their situation in normal language. The assistant detects useful signals and guides them toward relevant categories.

🌐

Three Languages

Designed for English, Kannada and Hindi so the platform can be easier to use across different communities.

📄

Document Readiness

The platform can highlight information or documents that users should verify before applying.

🔗

Official Source First

Scheme results are designed around official-source verification rather than promising eligibility.
