About ONEVISIT

One place to discover relevant opportunities and government support for your family.

The Problem

Families often miss schemes, loans, jobs, exams and other support because information is spread across different websites and departments.

Our Solution

ONEVISIT brings these opportunities together and filters them according to the person's age, occupation, situation and other profile details.

Key Idea

Instead of asking users to search through hundreds of schemes, ONEVISIT starts with the family and identifies potentially relevant support.

ONEVISIT is a hackathon prototype. Eligibility, deadlines, loan approval and market prices must always be verified from the official source.
