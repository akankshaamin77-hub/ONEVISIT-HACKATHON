About ONEVISIT

How the prototype works.

Family-based search

ONEVISIT lets a user enter family members and their circumstances. The prototype then checks the entered information against a set of example government schemes.

What is shown

Results show why a scheme appeared, information that still needs to be verified, and documents that may be required.

Additional tools

The prototype also includes loan calculation, jobs and internships, notifications, application priority, application locations, competitive exams and farmer market information.

Demo data is clearly identified where information normally requires a live government, employment, examination or market-data connection.
